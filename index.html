Faith Tycoon

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

Faith Tycoon is an idle clicker game. This policy explains, plainly, what happens to your information when you play it — and today, the short version is: your progress stays on your device, and we don't collect anything about you.

What we collect

Faith Tycoon does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. The app does not require an account, does not ask for your name, email, or location, and does not connect to any server of ours — there isn't one.

Where your progress lives

Your Faith, Grace, Gold, unlocked worlds, managers, and chests are saved locally on your own device, using your operating system's standard app-storage mechanism. This save data never leaves your device, is never seen by us, and is deleted automatically if you uninstall the app.

Third-party services

The current version of Faith Tycoon does not include advertising, analytics, or in-app purchases, so no third-party service receives any data from the app. If that changes in a future update — for example, adding optional rewarded ads — this policy will be updated first, and the new section will name the service and what it collects.

Children's privacy

Faith Tycoon does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Because the game requires no account and no personal information to play, there is nothing to collect regardless of the player's age.

Your device's own permissions

Faith Tycoon does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, photos, or location. Sound effects and music play using your device's normal audio output; nothing about your device's hardware is read or recorded.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes — most likely because a future version adds ads, analytics, or a cloud save — the "last updated" date above will change, and the new version will describe exactly what's collected and why.
