Faith Tycoon

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

Faith Tycoon is an idle clicker game. This policy explains, plainly, what happens to your information when you play it. Your progress itself always stays on your device — but the app does use a small number of well-known third-party services, described below, for crash reports, basic usage statistics, and optional rewarded ads.

What we collect directly

Faith Tycoon itself does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. The app does not require an account and does not ask for your name, email, or location. We don't operate our own server — the third-party services below run independently of us.

Where your progress lives

Your Faith, Grace, Gold, unlocked worlds, managers, and chests are saved locally on your own device, using your operating system's standard app-storage mechanism. This save data never leaves your device, is never seen by us, and is deleted automatically if you uninstall the app.

Third-party services

Faith Tycoon uses the following services, each covered by its own privacy policy:

Google AdMob — shows the optional "Watch Ad" rewarded ads (never required to play). AdMob may use a device identifier to serve ads; in the EU/UK, a consent form appears on first launch asking whether ads may be personalized, and you can change that choice anytime from Settings → Ad Privacy Choices. Outside those regions, or if you decline, ads are shown without personalization.

Firebase Analytics (Google) — collects basic, non-identifying usage data (which screens are opened, which in-game actions happen) so we can understand how the game is actually played.

Firebase Crashlytics (Google) — if the app crashes, sends a crash report (what happened, device type, OS version) so we can fix it. It does not include your save data or any personal information.

See Google's Privacy Policy for how Google's own services (AdMob, Firebase) handle data.

Children's privacy

Faith Tycoon does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children, and the game itself asks for none. Where required, ads are shown without personalization for users who indicate they are under the age of consent.

Your device's own permissions

Faith Tycoon does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, photos, or location. Sound effects and music play using your device's normal audio output; nothing about your device's hardware is read or recorded.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes — for example, if a future version adds a cloud save or in-app purchases — the "last updated" date above will change, and the new version will describe exactly what's collected and why.
