Common questions

Is my progress saved?
Yes — automatically, on your device. There's no account to lose access to.

Do I need an internet connection?
No, Faith Tycoon plays fully offline. An internet connection is only used to show the optional rewarded ads.

How do I report a bug?
Email the address above with what happened and, if you can, what device and iOS/Android version you're using.
